6 · Cookies and trackers

This site sets no cookies. None for advertising, none for analytics, none for social networks. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Two pieces of information are kept by your browser, and they never leave it:

  • the language you have chosen (FR or EN), so as not to ask you again on every page. Kept until you clear the site's data;
  • the fact that the intro animation has already played, so as not to play it again on every page. Cleared as soon as you close the tab.

Neither identifies you, nor allows you to be tracked from one site to another. They fall within the trackers exempt from consent under article 82 of the French Data Protection Act: the CNIL expressly cites interface personalisation trackers, including language choice. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings; the site will keep working — it will simply speak French to you again.

No analytics are in service on this site. We do not count visits, we do not know where you came from, and no statistics are produced about your visit.

The typefaces are hosted on this site: your browser does not contact Google to display them, and your IP address is not passed to Google. The display libraries are hosted here too. Viewing these pages therefore calls on no external service: everything your browser downloads comes from this domain. Only one third party can be contacted, and only if you act: Supabase, our host, when you send the contact form or open a trial. It sets no cookie.

The use of cookies within the application, which is a separate service with its own account, is detailed in the privacy policy.
