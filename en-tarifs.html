Frequently asked questions

You're probably wondering…

What does the free project include?
One complete project to get started: cockpit, signed decisions, messaging, documents, material library, client and consultant access. No credit card required. You move to the Studio plan when you create your second project, and everything you've built stays in place.
How does ALBA fit my current workflow?
ALBA adapts to your process, not the other way round. You configure the phases (concept, design, permits, tender, construction); we handle reminders, milestones and the project's memory. No lengthy training: most architects are up and running in under an hour.
Do my clients need to download an app?
No. ALBA runs entirely in the browser, on desktop and phone. A link, a password, your clients reach their cockpit in 30 seconds.
What happens to my data if I leave?
It's yours. At any time, export all your projects (PDF, ZIP, CSV) in one click. Your digital archives remain readable 10 years on.
Are decisions legally valid?
Every decision is timestamped, archived and electronically signed (eIDAS, simple level): the author, date and server timestamp are kept as evidence. For a document requiring an advanced or qualified signature, use your usual channel.
Can I invite my engineers and consultants?
Of course. Consultants get free access to the projects you invite them to, with the permission level you set (view, comment, upload).
How many team members are included?
The Discovery and Studio plans cover one person. The Practice plan goes up to four, at a decreasing rate from the second person onwards: the detail is on the Pricing page. Your clients and consultants remain unlimited and free — they count towards no plan.
What about the construction phase?
ALBA follows you on site: visit reports, photographed punch-list items assigned by trade, automatic distribution to contractors and the client. Every report is signed and archived, like a decision.
What file formats can I share?
All of them — PDF, DWG, IFC, images, videos, up to 100 MB per file. PDF plans and images open right in the browser: your clients don't need any software.
Where is my data hosted?
In France, with an ISO 27001-certified host: database, files and accounts. Encrypted at rest and in transit. Encrypted backups are kept within the European Union.
Are the prices shown excluding tax?
Yes, every amount on this page is exclusive of tax. Applicable VAT is calculated at checkout, based on your country and status: 20% for a practice registered in France. If you have an EU VAT number, you will be asked for it during signup. Your invoice is issued automatically after each payment.
How long does it take to get started?
You can start today. Account creation takes 3 minutes; importing your active projects takes half a day on average. We help with onboarding at no charge.
