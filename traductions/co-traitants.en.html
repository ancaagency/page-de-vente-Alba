Who pays for what

This is the question we are asked most often, and it deserves an answer at a glance. ALBA distinguishes two roles, and only one of them is billed.

Role Who Price How many
Collaborator Someone at the practice that holds the subscription €15/month excl. VAT, paid by the practice 4 per workspace
Guest Engineering consultant, sub-consultant, contractor, client Free Unlimited

If you received an invitation, you are on the second row. No payment card will be asked of you, now or later. Nor does the architect who invites you pay anything for you: the number of guests does not count towards their subscription.

What you can do

The architect sets your permissions, project by project. Depending on what they have chosen, you can:

  • view the project's drawings and documents in your browser, without downloading anything;
  • upload your own items — calculation notes, construction drawings, technical sheets — without a file transfer that expires;
  • comment, ask a question, answer a request for approval;
  • follow the decisions that concern you, with their date and their author.

You only see the projects you have been invited to, and nothing else. The practice's other work is not visible to you.

What becomes of your documents

A calculation note uploaded to ALBA remains yours. It is hosted in France, with an ISO 27001-certified provider, encrypted at rest and in transit. It is not resold, not analysed, and not used to train anything.

It is visible to the people the architect has invited to that project — which is rather the point. It is visible to no one else.

The part you only think about afterwards

What you upload stays available for as long as the project lives, and it is time-stamped. Six months later, when someone needs to know who sent which version and when, the answer is still there — for you too, not only for the architect.

In practice

  • You receive a link by email. You click, you choose a password, you are in.
  • Everything happens in the browser, on a computer as on a phone. Nothing to install.
  • There is a mobile app, but it is optional: it accompanies an account, it does not replace one.
  • If you no longer want access, tell the architect: they remove it in one click.

Three questions we get asked

Will I have to pay one day?

No — not to access the projects you are invited to. A subscription only becomes useful if you want to run your own work in ALBA, with your own projects and your own clients. That is a different use, and it stays optional.

Can the architect see what I do?

They see what you upload and what you write on the project, with the date — that is what a shared workspace is. They see nothing else: neither your activity elsewhere, nor your other projects.

And when the project ends?

The architect keeps their project and its archives. If you need a copy of what you uploaded, ask them: the export is complete and takes one click.
